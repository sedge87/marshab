## Batch 4 · Warnings that teach (L, design first)
26. **Habitat alerts, Subnautica-style:**
    - A calm voice-style alert line and a chime when something starts going wrong, e.g. "Oxygen falling: 19.8% and dropping. Check the oxygen generator's power."
    - Escalating: advisory, then warning, then critical.
    - Each alert names the cause and what it connects to, so students see the systems linking up (power → heating → crew health).
    - Needed before harder missions.

## Batch 5 · The mission dossier (L, design first)
27. **Mission log:**
    - Every mission's goal, versions tested, final design, graphs, what failed and what fixed it, and stretch goals.
    - Carried through the campaign and readable from Mission Control.
    - Exportable as a printable page (PDF or HTML) for the student's portfolio and the showcase.

## Batch 6 · Bigger features (L, design first)
28. **Observation cameras:**
    - One per room, a limited number, placed from a Mission Control panel.
    - You tab through them in Mission Control, and they can feed a wall screen.
29. **GAR-Y orders:** "Survey there", "Fetch the sample jars", "Come here", "Go home", from a small radial menu or Mission Control.
30. **Missions that use GAR-Y:** surveying for ice before you build an extractor, or fetching a sample for the lab.
31. **Ten missions, not six:**
    - Bigger and harder later missions that make students use the whole base, including the medical bay, and think about how the systems connect.
    - Built around the real hazards NASA lists for Mars crews: radiation, isolation, distance from Earth, gravity and a hostile, closed environment.
    - Candidates: a medical emergency, a solar particle event (shelter), a micrometeoroid hull breach, a water-system contamination, a comms blackout at solar conjunction, an equipment failure with spares, and crew morale over a long stay.
32. **Showcase mode:** present a finished base (it was already on the phase-3 list).

## Suggested order
**Batches 1 and 2 together** (fixes and quick wins), **then 3**. Then **4 → 5 → 6**, each starting from a short design for your approval. Batch 4 (alerts) comes before the new missions because harder missions need the base to tell students what's going wrong.
