# Controls

Click the Mars scene to capture the mouse, then use WASD to move and the mouse to look. Hold Shift to sprint and press Space to jump. Press E near an airlock or bay door to cycle it. Press Escape at any time to release the mouse and open the pause screen.

Press B to switch to Build mode. Choose a zone in the catalogue (Tab shows or hides it), then click or drag on the blueprint grid; the hologram turns green when the placement is valid and red with a reason when it is not. R rotates an airlock, hangar or solar face, right mouse cancels, X switches to the remove tool (full cargo refund) and Ctrl+Z undoes the last placement. WASD pans, Q/E orbit and the mouse wheel zooms the build camera.

Press M in either mode to open Mission Control: cargo, readiness for the crew and a plan of the habitat, with a suggested next step.
