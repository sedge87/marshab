# The opening slice: touchdown to the landing rehearsal (22 Nov 2026)

*From the user's 22 Nov narrative brief. The plan was agreed the same day. This is canon for the opening. It replaces the story and chapter order in `docs/opening-days.md` and `docs/intro-rework.md`, and the parts of `docs/master-design.md` §2 and §7 about the fried beacon and the player-built pad. The reactive-radio rules in `docs/intro-rework.md` §3 still apply. Every number is a starting point for playtest, and nothing here is sacred.*

## 1. The goal and the test
**Build goal:** a playable game from touchdown to the landing rehearsal, ending when the Pathfinder calls the hold. By then the player has:
- built a first base;
- expanded it enough to unlock one useful capability;
- used that capability in the field.

**The test:** can I build a place that feels like home, use something I added to it to solve a real problem in the field, and understand why I'm the one telling Castle to hold?

**The loop under test:** spot a problem in the field → come home with a reason to build → install a capability → go back out and use it.

## 2. The mission and the crew
An international expedition has come to set up a long-duration research outpost on Home plain. The site was chosen because it's within reach of:
- exposed ice;
- an old lakebed;
- a deep canyon;
- volcanic ground.

Uncrewed cargo flights came first, bringing the walkers, supplies and heavy kit, and a storm scattered some of it.

All six crew travelled together on the orbiter, with two descent vehicles docked to it.
- **The Pathfinder (you)** takes the compact scout lander down alone. It can reach rough ground and serves as a lifepod.
- **The other five** stay up with Devan's crew lander. A propellant leak in transit cost it its diversion reserve. That leaves enough for **one safe descent and no diversion to the backup site**.

Going first was always the Pathfinder's job: walk the pad, bring the beacon online, get the first habitat running. It should take a few sols.

| Crew | Role in the game |
|---|---|
| **Mira "Castle" Castellanos**, commander (she/her) | Your main radio relationship. She gives the assignment, looks after the crew and decides when they land. By the rehearsal she trusts your judgment on the ground. |
| **Devan Rao**, pilot, climate | Rehearses the descent; reads wind, dust and local conditions. |
| **Tamsin Achterberg**, engineer, geology | Reads radar, terrain and materials; advises on the beacon; keeps the orbiter's failing water recycler going. |
| **Joseph Mwangi**, doctor, human exploration | Looks after the crew in orbit; works out what they need to land, travel and live on the surface. |
| **Kai Tupou**, biologist, life | Reads samples and tests explanations, starting from evidence the player can see and collect. |

All five already speak in the radio scripts: Castle in the calls, the other four in the sunset-talk notes. The brief gives them bigger working parts. They know each other from the crossing, so let that come through, including ordinary talk on the drive home or while working at base.

**Voices:**
- Castle is the near voice.
- The other four come on when their speciality is needed, in short calls.
- The Earth Mission Control cast (`docs/mission-control-voice.md`) steps back to letters, or is cut. This is still open.

## 3. Where things are (proposal, from `docs/terrain/landmarks.md`)
- **The scout lander:** west Home plain (#1).
- **The planned pad:** east Home plain, about 0.4 km from the lander and 0.2 km north-west of the **Collapse pit (#7)**.
  - The pit is a fallen-in roof of the same void that runs under the pad: a known void to compare against.
- **The replacement candidate:** a bench across the **Dry channel (#3)**. Its eastern edge sits over the hidden return.
  - The void carries on east toward the **East front (#11)** lava step. That direction is the lead the slice ends on.
- **The near cargo:** within walking distance of the hatch. That's why the Pathfinder landed here.
  - GAR-Y's cradle at about 150 m;
  - three pallets;
  - Walker 1 in view, stalled 400–600 m out.
- **The Print Shop pallet:** in sight from the pad.

## 4a. Scenes, agreed one by one

### Scene 1: waking to the first step (about 3 min) · agreed 22 Nov
- **Opening (A1):** you wake after touchdown, strapped in. Black screen, then static as the link comes back, then Castle. *Later polish (A2): play the last minute of the descent: engines, dust, touchdown, silence.*
- **The assignment:** the exchange from §4, word for word, then: "Manifest's on your slate. See what came down near you."
- **The slate** is clipped by the seat. Unbuckle, take it, and it opens on the Cargo manifest.
- **The manifest (B1):** every container numbered: pallets 1–12, Walkers 1–3, GAR-Y's cradle.
  - The near ones are marked "in your area", the rest "location unknown".
  - All contents show "unknown".
  - A few entries are corrupted, for story beats later.
- **Helmet and hatch:** seal the helmet; the hatch cycles and vents. The existing hatch refusal stays.
- **First step:** the white-out, the boot print, then the view:
  - a parachute on a low rise;
  - GAR-Y's empty cradle about 150 m away, with fresh tracks leading off;
  - the pad's thin mast far to the east, looking fine from here.
  - A Notes thought: *That's GAR-Y's cradle. Empty.*
- **Meeting GAR-Y (C1):** follow its tracks from the cradle. It's working about 100 m past it, scanning, and rolls over when it sees you. All within the first 5 minutes.

### Scene 2: meeting GAR-Y (about minutes 3–6) · agreed 22 Nov
- **Follow the stakes, not tracks.** From the empty cradle a line of small orange survey stakes leads off: GAR-Y has been working on its own since it woke. This plants the survey-stake idea early.
- **It's tangled (A1).** A drifting canopy has snagged its wheels and mast. It strains and chirps unhappily. You free it by hand at two or three snag points (hold E at each) while it watches. *New small interaction; uses the existing canopy pieces.*
- **The held moment (B1, as designed 7 Nov).**
  - The HUD fades, you can't walk, and your view is drawn gently to GAR-Y for about 20 s.
  - The head turns, finds you and stops. A questioning chirp; it rolls over, circles you once, spins happily and parks at your side.
  - The music swells, "GAR-Y" fades up once, and the Notes thought: *I'm not alone out here.*
- **Castle reacts through your helmet camera** with warmth, not instructions ("Is that… GAR-Y's alive? Oh, good. Hi, GAR-Y.").
- **GAR-Y leads first (C1).** It beeps, looks at you, rolls to the nearest pallet's beacon, and waits there looking back. It knows the manifest's pallets.
  - At the pallet, its order menu appears for the first time, with a one-off prompt: "Hold G: GAR-Y".
- **Kept:** if you black out before meeting it, GAR-Y finds you and you wake with it at the hatch.

### Scene 3: the near pallets (about minutes 6–15) · agreed 22 Nov
- GAR-Y leads you to the nearest pallet, on the low rise. From the top you see the other two chutes and Walker 1's amber lamp far out.
- **Opening:** free four latches by hand, and the lid comes off. The manifest updates on the spot: contents revealed, first tick "found". The second tick, "recovered", comes once it's home.
- **Three pallets (A1):**
  - **stores:** rations, water, the sample kit with the hand corer, a bundle of survey stakes;
  - **power module:** the core's power and basic life support, one heavy unit;
  - **tools:** hammer, headlamp, patch kit, two spares kits, **GAR-Y's hitch bracket**.
- **The hitch (C1):** the bracket fits GAR-Y's rear mount. It's the first use of equipment fitting, and it stays visible on GAR-Y.
- **The power module wakes Walker 1 (P1, agreed).**
  - The walker stalled on a flat battery after weeks of storm dust, plus a failed wheel seal.
  - GAR-Y tows the module to it on the new hitch (or you carry it, slowly). You plug it into the walker's frame socket and the lamp goes from amber to steady.
  - The module rides home inside the walker and becomes the core's power when it folds. One object, two jobs.
- **Kit and equipment (agreed).**
  - **A Kit page on the slate:** what you carry (the field kit slots, the sample case, any heavy load and its weight against your margin), the suit's add-ons, and GAR-Y's fittings.
  - **Fitting is physical:** suit add-ons at a suit rack (the lander's, then the core's); GAR-Y's at its mounts.
  - **Tools in the hand are chosen from the kit slots with keys 1–4**, so it's always clear what you're holding. This replaces using tools automatically by context.

### Scene 4: Walker 1 (about minutes 15–28) · agreed 22 Nov
- **Getting the power to it:** fit the hitch bracket to GAR-Y at the tools pallet (the first fitting, visible at once). GAR-Y tows the power module 400–600 m toward the amber lamp, and you walk alongside, minding your margin.
- **Up close:** crouched, dust-caked, one leg sagging on a flat wheel.
- **Waking it:** plug the module into its frame socket. The lamp goes steady, the joints twitch, and the deck screen comes on (a working display, so text is fine there).
- **The fault, found with your senses (A1):** the screen says only "pressure loss, front axle". You walk the front wheels and spot a thin dust plume and frost on the bad seal, then patch it. *We're the Pathfinder.*
- **Navigation (B2):** no sun-sight fix. Its navigation comes back with power. *The sun-sight can return for a walker found far off.*
- **Overnight (C1):** it follows you back and kneels beside the scout lander, lamp steady. It's the last thing you see from the window on sol 1, and at dawn it's ready to ride.

### Scene 5: sunset and the first night in the lander (about 5 min) · agreed 22 Nov
- **Dusk:** back at the lander, with Walker 1 kneeling beside it and GAR-Y parked by the steps. Go in, cycle the hatch, take the helmet off: a small warm cabin, the fans ticking.
- **The evening call:** the whole crew for the first time. Each speaks once about **what you found today**, not their job:
  - Devan: the afternoon dust;
  - Tamsin: "Tell me that walker's seal held";
  - Joseph: "Eat something, please";
  - Kai: a dig about GAR-Y;
  - Castle closes with Thursday.
- **Before bed, free:** eat a ration by hand, read the manifest, look out at the walker's lamp, write the journal.
- **The soft forced sleep (A1):** after dark on sol 1 the hatch won't open. Castle: "No night walks on day one. Humour me." You can stay up as long as you like; sleep comes when you strap into the seat, or once tiredness wins.
- **The night (B1):** as you drift off, the walker's servos shift outside and GAR-Y chirps once. Fade, then dawn.

### Scene 6: the dawn ride and choosing home (sol 2, about 8 min) · agreed 22 Nov
- **Dawn:** Walker 1 kneels by the lander with frost on its frame. You climb the ladder to the deck, grip the rail, and it stands.
- **The ride:** you stand on the deck and steer by pointing it. GAR-Y trots alongside. The plain looks different from 4 m up, and the pad's mast is in view to the east.
- **Choosing home: stand on the spot (A1).** The deck screen reads the ground beneath: **ground** (firm, slope, soft), **sun** (hours, so power), **shelter** (wind, dust), **water** (distance to ice).
  - Near the lander is allowed, but it reads as plain. Walking further earns better numbers.
  - Cores refold without limit, so a first choice is a lesson, not a trap.
- **Where (B1):** within about 1 km of the planned pad, on ground that passes the existing site check.
- **The set-down (C1 with a preview):** "Set down here" shows a faint outline of the core and airlock on the ground. You turn to aim the airlock, confirm, and the fold plays uninterrupted in low morning light. GAR-Y circles it once.

### Building rule · agreed 22 Nov
- **Structure** (rooms/liners, corridors, airlocks, the pad) is planned only at a **Site plan terminal**. It opens today's build view over the whole site, and the crawler prints in real time.
  - **The player places the terminal themselves.** It comes in the walker's fit-out kit, and every core's kit has one. Suggested spot: by the airlock and suit rack, so you pass it going out and coming in.
- **Furnishings and outdoor kit are placed by hand**, anywhere sensible. You hold the item you're actually carrying (from your kit or the base's store), a faint preview shows, R turns it, and you set it down.
- **The slate shows the plan, read-only** (a Site page).
- **Later:** a terminal in the Workshop, so big bases are never far from one.

### Scene 7: the first indoor moment (sol 2 morning, about 8 min) · agreed 22 Nov
- **First time inside the core:** rigid, bare, cold and dim. Life support is already running on the power module: the vents hum and the window frost clears. Power + hull, shown.
- **The fit-out kit, placed by hand (A1):** bunk, rack, table, chair, work light and the Site plan terminal, in crates by the wall. You place each piece with a preview and R to turn it.
  - The work light changes the room as soon as it's down.
  - You choose where the terminal goes.
- **The first room:** at your terminal, plan the first Quarters liner against the core. It's free (in the kit), with a one-off prompt.
  - Watch through the window as the liner inflates and the crawler lays the shell.
  - Then place the bunk inside it.
- **Helmet off in the Quarters (B1):** once it's sealed and its air reads green. GAR-Y potters in and inspects.
- **The quiet call, Castle alone:** "Describe it to me. What can you see?" A reply choice, something human from her, then: "Right. Pad's this afternoon. Devan's already drawing on the window."
- **The base store starts small:** what came on the walker plus your pallet finds.

### Act 4: a site that feels like the answer (sols 3–4, about 35 min)
14. **The candidate.** Joseph's criteria plus the first sled lines point to the bench across the Dry channel.
15. **The route decision: the dust and visibility system.**
    - **The north crossing:** short and exposed. Its sand floor lifts dust and cuts visibility as the wind picks up after midday.
    - **The south crossing:** banked, sheltered and longer, so it costs suit margin.
    - You go early, take the long way, or pin your way back.
    - If you've fixed the Scout buggy (the spares-kit fix is built), you can drive. North sand costs it charge.
16. **The reading the conditions change: the wind logger.**
    - In the lee of the bench it reads calm. That's wrong, but plausible.
    - Placed exposed, the two traces disagree. Devan explains only after you've seen it.
    - Moving it fixes a bad placement.
17. **The sled survey.**
    - Stake a line, and GAR-Y tows the sled: solid ground.
    - The sled can't take the bench's edge step, so the end of the profile is **unresolved** (hatched).
    - **Kai, optional:** a core near the edge drops through a soft layer. This is a new "dropped through" result on the hand corer. He's curious; nobody is alarmed.
18. **The beacon test.**
    - Carry the repaired beacon to the bench, set its feet, connect it, power it.
    - Devan locks on from orbit. Castle and Devan sound hopeful: "Thursday's back on."

### Act 5: the rehearsal and the hold (sols 4–5, about 10 min)
19. **The ground rehearsal.** Devan talks through the approach live over the transmitting beacon.
20. **The latest survey shows a return past the edge you'd marked.** You either extend the line round the step from below, or verify against known-solid ground. Either way the void carries on under the far edge of the approach.
21. **The call: the same result, played out differently (decided).** It always ends with **you** saying "Castle, hold", and Castle backing it. There are three paths, chosen by live facts:
    - **You checked first:** you open the rehearsal with the hold. It's calm; Devan is disappointed but grateful.
    - **You went in unsure:** GAR-Y's live pass draws the return mid-countdown. You call it under pressure.
    - **You said "we're good":** at Devan's final check, the beacon's tilt sensor or the latest profile flags it. You make the call at the last second. "Good call. Late, but good."

    On every path the evidence is on screen before the reply is offered. The slice ends on one lead: **the void runs east, toward the East front lava.**

**Total:** about 2½–3 hours of play.
