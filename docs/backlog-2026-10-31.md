3. **Sleeping quarters as their own room type,** separate from living quarters. Players keep building noisy rooms next to living quarters because the game more or less forces it.
4. **Rethink the order bases get built in,** and how that works mechanically with the survival mechanics, which need a full rework too.
5. **Windows on inner walls,** behaving differently: no effect on heat and so on.
6. **Ctrl+Z doesn't work as intended.**
