## 4. A working prototype of the alerts, to make decision 4.1 easier
It's on its own branch, **`prototype/habitat-alerts`** (commit `358a2eb`), not on main. **Look at:** `docs/prototypes/alert1.jpg` (a critical banner with its cause chain) and `alert2.jpg` (the active alerts as pills under the clock). To play with it, check out the branch:

```
git switch prototype/habitat-alerts
```

What it already does:
- advisory, warning and critical alerts for power, batteries before sunset, oxygen, CO₂, cold rooms, water, food and crew health;
- a chime per level;
- the cause chain (for example: power → oxygen generator → O₂ falling → crew health);
- repeats while an alert lasts, and a red edge pulse for critical alerts;
- an "all clear" when it's fixed.

Still to come once you've decided: the voice, the "What's happening?" explainer cards, the Mission Control log tab, and the list of alerts in the test report.
