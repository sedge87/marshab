# Rooms and equipment (14 Nov 2026)

*Agreed with the user on 14 Nov 2026, the second design talk after `docs/pillars.md` and `docs/range-ladder.md`. Where `docs/systems-and-items.md` §3 to §5 disagree, this wins. Numbers, plan lists and which station each find needs are filled in when each room is built.*

## The rule

Every room and every piece of equipment helps you do one of three things:
- **go further;**
- **understand what you found;**
- **bring the crew down.**

Rooms only ever **help**. There are no noise penalties, no cramped-work penalties and no layout puzzles.

## Every base: the core

The walker's core is the only rigid Earth module. Each of the four walkers brings one, and it sets up the base where it kneels.
- **Airlock and suit rack:** choose your add-ons for the trip, and refill air and battery. The suit goes on and off in a couple of seconds.
- **Life support:** ice melter, then water, then oxygen. Its panel shows the chain.
- **Power hub:** batteries and the power panel.
- **A poor bunk:** you can sleep here, but Quarters sleep better.

### Core upgrades open room types

- Each base's core can be upgraded. An upgrade is a **found plan plus materials, fitted at that base's core**, and it opens room types **at that base**.
- **There's no limit:** any base can take every upgrade. Outposts end up specialised only because of what you choose to fit there.

| Upgrade | Opens |
|---|---|
| **Water loop** | Galley, Greenhouse |
| **Heavy power** | Workshop, big outside machines |
| **Lab services** | Lab |
| **Vehicle charging** | Garage |
| **Shielding** | Shelter |

**Example:** your main base can't feed a Greenhouse yet. You find the water-loop plan in a wreck, print it and fit it to the core. Galley and Greenhouse are now on that base's build bar.

## Room types: plan by type, place freely

- **Plan by type.** When you draw a room in Plan view, you give it a type, and it's drawn in that type's colour, so you can plan the whole base at a glance. Builders asked for this.
- **Place freely.** The room's **key piece** (the Lab's analyser, the Quarters' bunk) arrives with it, in a sensible spot that you can move. You place every other station and extra wherever you like inside.
- **The room's bonus works while its key piece is inside.**

## Workflows: hands-on first, then automatic

Some rooms are a short chain of stations.

| Room | The chain |
|---|---|
| **Lab** | rock saw, then microscope, then spectrometer, then later stations |
| **Greenhouse** | regolith washer, then planters, then harvest to the Galley |
| **Workshop** | smelter, then Print Shop, then repair bench |
| **Core** | ice melter, then water, then oxygen |

- **Each job needs the stations it needs.** Some take one station, some take several, depending on the sample or the task.
  - A plain rock needs only the saw and the microscope.
  - A cave crust needs the spectrometer as well.
  - With fewer stations than a find needs, you get a partial read: "something's here, but I can't tell what".
- **The first time you meet a new kind of find or job, you do each step by hand.** That's when it's interesting.
- **After that it runs by itself,** so the twentieth rock is never a chore (the chore check, systems-and-items §10g).
- **New stations come from found plans**, often sent by an owner in orbit after a find.

## The crew in orbit and the rooms

Each owner looks after some room types and upgrades:

| Owner | Looks after |
|---|---|
| **Castle** (the base and the landing) | the core and its upgrades, the Garage, the landing pad |
| **Tupou** (Life) | the Lab's later stations, the Greenhouse |
| **Rao** (Climate) | instruments, relay beacons, the Shelter's shielding |
| **Achterberg** (Geology) | the Workshop, the smelter, the miner, the extractor |
| **Mwangi** (Humans) | Quarters, the Galley, the Shelter's first aid |

- **They send plans as story beats** when you find something. For example, you bring back your first layered clay, and Tupou sends the microscope's plan with a note: "Look at it properly, please."
- **They react to how you build**, on the radio through Castle and in their slate notes.

## Outside equipment

| Group | Pieces |
|---|---|
| **Power and printing** | solar arrays and battery banks, later a compact reactor (like NASA's Kilopower); the regolith printer, a tracked crawler that prints shells, pads and berms |
| **Mining and hauling** | the ice extractor and the rock miner on deposits, each filling a hopper; a cart dock; hauler carts that run your trails |
| **Signal and the landing** | relay beacons (radio into canyons and caves), trail light stakes, and the crew's landing pad with its lights and beacon |
| **Science instruments** | placed in the field, they gather data over sols. Placement matters: better spots give better data. See the list below. |

The science instruments:
- **weather station:** storm forecasts;
- **seismometer:** quakes, and hints of hollow ground;
- **radiation monitor:** solar-storm warnings;
- **magnetometer:** maps the heart's field;
- **heat-flow probe:** finds warm ground.

## How building feels

- **Instant, with a short "watch it build" moment** when you place a piece from the build menu, a few seconds at most:
  - a room: the liner inflates, and the crawler makes a quick pass laying the shell;
  - equipment: it unfolds;
  - outside kit: it settles into place.
- Then it works at once. There's no waiting and no carrying parts.
- What holds you back is **plans and materials**, never time.
