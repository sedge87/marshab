# Review checklist — General Habitat Equipment

- [ ] All seven GLBs import at 1:1 metre scale in Godot.
- [ ] Fire extinguisher is visibly retained in its bracket; first-aid drawer and alarm lights appear correct.
- [ ] Emergency locker contains visible emergency supplies and does not clip with its door.
- [ ] First aid kit handle and latches remain attached at all view distances.
- [ ] Portable light stands on the floor and its cord connects the power unit and lamps.
- [ ] Work-light real-time light toggles and stays within performance budget.
- [ ] Crate variants are recognisably different sizes, with no floating latches.
- [ ] Props can be used in at least two different interior zones.
- [ ] Room-specific and general placement rules do not conflict.
- [ ] Simple collision allows walking around the equipment.
- [ ] Scene saves and reloads without losing transforms/interactions.
- [ ] No static false data displays, mirrored letters or missing materials.
- [ ] Original catalogue objects and room modules are preserved as backups.
