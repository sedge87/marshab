# Controls

Click the Mars scene to capture the mouse, then use WASD to move and the mouse to look. Hold Shift to sprint and press Space to jump. Press E near an airlock or bay door to cycle it. Press F to toggle your helmet head lamp; it uses no habitat or grid power (when looking at equipment with an F action, that contextual action takes priority). Press Escape at any time to release the mouse and open the pause screen.

Building has two views, and B picks the right one for where you stand. Outside, B opens the **Site plan**: an overhead blueprint for rooms, corridors, airlocks and power. Inside a room, B opens **Interior**: first-person furnishing of that room. Shift+B swaps between the two, so you can add an airlock or more rooms without walking out; from Explore, Shift+B always opens the Site plan. Both views show a Site plan | Interior | Explore switcher at the top, and B returns to Explore.

In the Site plan, choose a zone in the catalogue (Tab shows or hides it), then click or drag on the blueprint grid; the hologram turns green when the placement is valid and red with a reason when it is not. R rotates an airlock, hangar or solar face, right mouse or Esc cancels, X switches to the remove tool (full cargo refund) and Ctrl+Z undoes the last placement. WASD pans, Q/E orbit and the mouse wheel zooms the build camera.

Press V with a room selected to draw an angled room: click cell corners (walls run straight or at 45°), then click the first corner again or press Enter to close it; Backspace removes the last corner. Angled rooms behave like grid rooms: they can share walls with any room, a grid corridor or airlock meeting one of their straight walls opens into them, and a corridor started on a 45° wall can run on into another room's 45° wall. Rooms that touch join automatically: two rooms of the same type (any shape) merge into one room, and a new room touching a different type gets one door in the middle of their shared wall. Doors show as bars on the site plan; in Interior, X then aim at a door removes it (sealing that wall), and the door tool adds more. Undo after a merge returns the earlier room to its old shape.

In Interior, Tab opens the kit (1–5 pick quick items), aim at a floor, wall or ceiling and left-click to place; R or the mouse wheel turns the item, X removes the item you aim at (full refund), and removing a room in the Site plan also removes and refunds its equipment. The kit's **Window** tool glazes one outside wall bay of a room or corridor: aim at the wall (the 3 m bay highlights green or red) and left-click. Each window costs 150 kg of cargo and adds heating draw, because glass loses heat faster than the hull. X removes a window, Ctrl+Z undoes the last one, and windows show as pale blue bars on the site plan. Airlock and vehicle-bay walls stay solid, and a window is removed (and refunded) if its wall stops facing Mars.

Press M in either mode to open Mission Control: cargo, readiness for the crew and a plan of the habitat, with a suggested next step.

In **Missions**, the mission panel (top right) lists the requirements. Press **T** to test your design: it fast-forwards the mission (nights, storms and all) on a copy of your base and shows a report saying what passed, what failed, when and why, with graphs. Every test is saved as a version. Press **J** for the mission brief, hints and your versions so far. A passed test lets you complete the mission.

New games start inside the crew lander: look at the hatch and press E to open it, then walk down the ramp. Before landing, the **Land** step lets you choose where the lander comes down: click the map to set the target. In Survival the lander touches down somewhere inside the landing ellipse; in Freedom and Creative it lands exactly on target.
