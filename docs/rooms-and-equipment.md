# Rooms and equipment (14 Nov 2026)

*Agreed with the user on 14 Nov 2026, the second design talk after `docs/pillars.md` and `docs/range-ladder.md`. Where `docs/systems-and-items.md` §3 to §5 disagree, this wins. Numbers, plan lists and which station each find needs are filled in when each room is built.*

## The rule

Every room and every piece of equipment helps you do one of three things:
- **go further;**
- **understand what you found;**
- **bring the crew down.**

Rooms only ever **help**. There are no noise penalties, no cramped-work penalties and no layout puzzles.

## Every base: the core

The walker's core is the only rigid Earth module. Each of the four walkers brings one, and it sets up the base where it kneels.
- **Airlock and suit rack:** choose your add-ons for the trip, and refill air and battery. The suit goes on and off in a couple of seconds.
- **Life support:** ice melter, then water, then oxygen. Its panel shows the chain.
- **Power hub:** batteries and the power panel.
- **A poor bunk:** you can sleep here, but Quarters sleep better.

### Core upgrades open room types

- Each base's core can be upgraded. An upgrade is a **found plan plus materials, fitted at that base's core**, and it opens room types **at that base**.
- **There's no limit:** any base can take every upgrade. Outposts end up specialised only because of what you choose to fit there.

| Upgrade | Opens |
|---|---|
| **Water loop** | Galley, Greenhouse |
| **Heavy power** | Workshop, big outside machines |
| **Lab services** | Lab |
| **Vehicle charging** | Garage |
| **Shielding** | Shelter |

**Example:** your main base can't feed a Greenhouse yet. You find the water-loop plan in a wreck, print it and fit it to the core. Galley and Greenhouse are now on that base's build bar.

## Room types: plan by type, place freely

- **Plan by type.** When you draw a room in Plan view, you give it a type, and it's drawn in that type's colour, so you can plan the whole base at a glance. Builders asked for this.
- **Place freely.** The room's **key piece** (the Lab's analyser, the Quarters' bunk) arrives with it, in a sensible spot that you can move. You place every other station and extra wherever you like inside.
- **The room's bonus works while its key piece is inside.**

## Workflows: hands-on first, then automatic

Some rooms are a short chain of stations.

| Room | The chain |
|---|---|
| **Lab** | rock saw, then microscope, then spectrometer, then later stations |
| **Greenhouse** | regolith washer, then planters, then harvest to the Galley |
| **Workshop** | smelter, then Print Shop, then repair bench |
| **Core** | ice melter, then water, then oxygen |

- **Each job needs the stations it needs.** Some take one station, some take several, depending on the sample or the task.
  - A plain rock needs only the saw and the microscope.
  - A cave crust needs the spectrometer as well.
  - With fewer stations than a find needs, you get a partial read: "something's here, but I can't tell what".
- **The first time you meet a new kind of find or job, you do each step by hand.** That's when it's interesting.
- **After that it runs by itself,** so the twentieth rock is never a chore (the chore check, systems-and-items §10g).
- **New stations come from found plans**, often sent by an owner in orbit after a find.

## The crew in orbit and the rooms

Each owner looks after some room types and upgrades:

| Owner | Looks after |
|---|---|
| **Castle** (the base and the landing) | the core and its upgrades, the Garage, the landing pad |
| **Tupou** (Life) | the Lab's later stations, the Greenhouse |
| **Rao** (Climate) | instruments, relay beacons, the Shelter's shielding |
| **Achterberg** (Geology) | the Workshop, the smelter, the miner, the extractor |
| **Mwangi** (Humans) | Quarters, the Galley, the Shelter's first aid |

- **They send plans as story beats** when you find something. For example, you bring back your first layered clay, and Tupou sends the microscope's plan with a note: "Look at it properly, please."
- **They react to how you build**, on the radio through Castle and in their slate notes.

## Outside equipment

| Group | Pieces |
|---|---|
| **Power and printing** | solar arrays and battery banks, later a compact reactor (like NASA's Kilopower); the regolith printer, a tracked crawler that prints shells, pads and berms |
| **Mining and hauling** | the ice extractor and the rock miner on deposits, each filling a hopper; a cart dock; hauler carts that run your trails |
| **Signal and the landing** | relay beacons (radio into canyons and caves), trail light stakes, and the crew's landing pad with its lights and beacon |
| **Science instruments** | placed in the field, they gather data over sols. Placement matters: better spots give better data. See the list below. |

The science instruments:
- **weather station:** storm forecasts;
- **seismometer:** quakes, and hints of hollow ground;
- **radiation monitor:** solar-storm warnings;
- **magnetometer:** maps the heart's field;
- **heat-flow probe:** finds warm ground.

## How building feels

- **Instant, with a short "watch it build" moment** when you place a piece from the build menu, a few seconds at most:
  - a room: the liner inflates, and the crawler makes a quick pass laying the shell;
  - equipment: it unfolds;
  - outside kit: it settles into place.
- Then it works at once. There's no waiting and no carrying parts.
- What holds you back is **plans and materials**, never time.

## As built (A3, 14 Nov 2026)

*What the builder decided where this doc left things open. Code: `scripts/room_types.gd` (the room model), `scripts/core_upgrades_screen.gd` and `scripts/core_panel.gd` (fitting upgrades), `scripts/room_build_moment.gd` (the build moment).*

- **The ids stay, so saves keep working.** `general` is now the walker core ("Walker Core" on the bar), `engineering` the Workshop, `science` the Lab, `living` Quarters, `hydroponics` the Greenhouse, `medical` the Shelter, `vehicle_bay` the Garage; `galley` and `storage` keep their names.
- **Cut room types:** Life Support, Hygiene and Mission Operations (and Waste Management, cut on 31 Oct). Their kit moved:
  - life-support kit fits the core and the Workshop;
  - hygiene kit fits Quarters (the toilet and washbasin also fit the core);
  - comms kit fits the Lab (the comms station also fits the core);
  - older saves turn those rooms into the Workshop, Quarters and the Lab.
- **The core** is the carried module of the opening, unchanged in shape and in how it's set down, with the same fit-out kit (its rack holds the life-support and power switches). Once there's a walker in the world, the core can't be drawn by hand; Creative still can. Its bunk is the "poor" one: no penalty, but Quarters sleep better.
- **Key pieces:** Workshop, the Print Shop; Lab, the Science Workbench; Quarters, a bunk; Galley, the kitchenette; Greenhouse, a planter; Storage, a cargo rack; Garage, the rover charger; Shelter, a bunk (the "shielded bunk"). A new room places its key piece itself: a bunk or counter against a wall, a bench or planter near the middle. It's free, worked out from the rooms like the crate copies. Remove it and it waits on the Unpacked tab, to put back wherever you like; remove the room and the free copy goes with it.
- **Bonuses built now:**
  - the Workshop's Print Shop prints 1.5 times as fast; the core's prints at full speed (the old 75 % was a penalty);
  - a night in Quarters (bunk inside) leaves you rested 1.5 times as long;
  - the Shelter (bunk inside) has twice the hull's shielding and counts as a storm shelter without a locker;
  - the Lab is where finds are analysed, and the Greenhouse grows food, as before.
  - **Later:** the Galley's food-bar packer and longer trips (A5), Storage holding more (with the shared store, A4), the Garage's faster turnaround (A7). Their cards show no bonus until then.
- **No noise or cramped penalties:** the noisy-room and noisy-item lists are empty, and the core gives the crew somewhere to work at full speed.
- **Core upgrades** are fitted with E on the panel beside the core rack's switches (its lamp is amber while a plan waits). Each costs feedstock as it's fitted; it isn't printed. Until wrecks hold plans (the range ladder), they come as story beats:

| Upgrade | Opens | Plan arrives | Feedstock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water loop | Galley, Greenhouse | the water step (water of your own) | 180 kg |
| Heavy power | Workshop | your first Print Shop job | 220 kg |
| Lab services | Lab | science opens (in the opening, the ice extractor) | 120 kg |
| Vehicle charging | Garage | sol 10 | 200 kg |
| Shielding | Shelter | sol 10 | 250 kg |

  - Quarters and Storage are open from the start.
  - Locked room types stay on the Rooms tab, greyed, marked CORE UPGRADE, with the reason.
  - The upgrades apply under chapter one's rules (new Survival and Freedom games). Creative and older saves have every room type open; a save from before A3 loads with every upgrade fitted.
  - Upgrades are kept per base ("main" for now), ready for A4.
- **The build moment:** placing a liner room (not the Garage's hangar) plays about three seconds over it: the liner inflates off the floor, then a crawler passes over the roof laying a regolith skin, and it fades. The room works at once.

## As built (A5, 15 Nov 2026)

*What the builder decided for room workflows and the four materials. Code: `scripts/materials.gd` (metal, salvage, ore, the smelter), `scripts/greenhouse.gd` (washed soil, the washer, the first harvest), `scripts/samples.gd` (the Lab's chain, by hand, partial reads), `scripts/habitat_wear.gd` (the repair bench), `scripts/station_model.gd` (the new stations' models). ### The four materials (the user chose to build them in A5)
- **Regolith = feedstock,** as before: the intake makes it (still 150 kg a sol; tune in playtest).
- **Ice = water,** as before.
- **Metal** comes from iron-rich rock through the Workshop's smelter.
- **Salvage** is Earth-made parts: 6 kg for each piece of lander debris you strip, 25 kg for each cargo pallet opened by hand.
- **Plans mix them.** Every plan in `data/plans.json` now has `metal_kg` and `salvage_kg` beside its feedstock (for example the airlock is 60 metal and 20 salvage, the surface reactor 120 and 80). Core upgrades and room fits pay metal and salvage too.
- The Print Shop takes them when a job starts, gives them back if it's cancelled, and charges extra copies when they settle. Short of one, the card says what's missing and what you have.
- **The store** shows feedstock, metal and salvage on the Print Shop, the build bar and the Slate's Base page. The Slate also shows ore at base and ore in the suit.
- **Starting stock:** 60 kg of metal and 60 kg of salvage, in the Print Shop's crate. A save from before A5 loads with 400 kg of each, so nothing stalls.

### Iron-rich rock
- **It can be broken off only once the Lab has named it:** a full read of hematite or an iron meteorite in the archive.
- **Hold E on a chipped rock of that kind** for 1.8 seconds to break off 10 kg. That's three break-offs a rock, and up to 30 kg in the suit.
- **Carried by hand until A8.** Inside a base, it goes into the store.
- **What it yields:** hematite gives half its weight in metal, a meteorite 0.8.

### The Lab: saw → microscope → spectrometer
- **New stations:** the Rock Saw and the Spectrometer, with code-built models in the house style (no text on them; a screen and a status light).
- **The rock saw is a brought plan,** 40 feedstock and 15 metal. The microscope and spectrometer plans come from finds.
- **What each kind needs.** A full read needs every station on its list:
  - **rock:** saw and microscope;
  - **soil:** microscope;
  - **ice core:** saw and microscope;
  - **each find:** its own `stations` list in `data/discoveries.json`. For example, perchlorate needs the microscope and spectrometer; hematite needs all three.
- **Partial reads.** With fewer stations, you get the findings the stations you have can see. The read is marked partial and says what it needs ("It needs a spectrometer"). It waits in the store to go again.
- **Tupou sends the plans.** The first partial read short of a station brings Tupou's plan for it, with a note: "Look at it properly, please." The first copy is printed free.
- **Hands-on the first time for each kind** (rock by its rock type, soil, ice core):
  - the Lab screen marks it "new kind" and offers "Do this one by hand";
  - you walk it round the stations in order, pressing E at each one ("Cut the sample", "Look at the grains", "Read the spectrum"), then E at the workbench: "Put the analysis together";
  - doing the stations out of order is refused;
  - from then on, the bench runs that kind by itself;
  - "Put the sample in hand back" cancels it.
- **Older saves** count every kind they've already analysed as learned, and get the microscope and spectrometer plans.

### The Workshop: smelter → Print Shop → repair bench
- **The smelter plan:**
  - Achterberg sends it after a hematite, meteorite or iron-dust find;
  - it costs 150 feedstock and 20 salvage;
  - it fits only the Workshop.
- **The first batch is smelted by hand:** E on the smelter melts 10 kg of ore, or 60 kg of regolith if there's no ore.
- **After that it smelts by itself** while powered: 40 kg of ore a sol.
- **With no ore, it smelts regolith** at 80 kg a sol and a low yield (0.08), but never takes the printers' last 300 kg.
- **The repair bench is the Engineering Bench** (`tool_bench`):
  - service a kind of machine by hand once (E with a spares kit);
  - after that, a bench in a Workshop services that kind on its rounds, from 50 % wear, using a kit each time.

### The Greenhouse: washer → planters → harvest
- **Planters need washed soil** under chapter rules: 40 kg each, once. Until then their status says "Waiting for washed soil". Older trays already have theirs.
- **The Regolith Washer plan:** Tupou sends it after the food step. It goes in the Greenhouse.
- **The washer by hand, then by itself:**
  - E on the washer washes the first 40 kg by hand: 40 kg of feedstock and about 10 L of water;
  - after that it keeps up to 80 kg ready, washing 60 kg a sol;
  - chapter one's food step ("Wash the regolith sample") also works at the washer.
- **The first harvest of each crop is by hand** ("First … harvested"). After that the harvest cart picks it.

### The core: melter → water → oxygen
- **Already a working chain:** the ice melter, the water tank and the oxygen generator, in the existing life-support loop. A5 doesn't change it.

### On the Slate
- The Base page lists each room's chain for the chosen base, with a tick for each station that's there:
  - Lab: saw → microscope → spectrometer;
  - Workshop: smelter → Print Shop → repair bench;
  - Greenhouse: washer → planter → cart.
