# The crew's stories (22 Nov 2026, in progress)

The orbiter crew now carry the game's human stories. That job used to belong to Mission Control (`docs/mission-control-voice.md`); what's usable from that cast moves here. A character session to flesh everyone out properly is still to come (the user, 22 Nov). Nothing here is built.

## 1. How the stories reach you
- **Like Firewatch: real conversations, anywhere.** The suit radio carries them, and the crew see your helmet camera, so some calls react to what you're looking at (a strange rock, a wreck, the view).
  - Each call is a back-and-forth with 2–3 reply choices, or saying nothing.
  - They remember your answers.
  - You can also ring someone from the base radio when you have something to tell them.
- **Interference cuts it in places.** Some areas break up or block the link (the Interference thread, master-design §7). Out there you're on your own.
- **Threads move forward when you do things in someone's field.**
  - Kai: samples you bring home.
  - Tamsin: things you build.
  - Devan: far drives and the weather.
  - Joseph: rest, injuries, music.
  - Castle: her thread runs on trust and time.
  What you choose to do decides whose story moves forward.
- **Bonds are light and written in.** Reply choices are remembered, and lines refer back to them (Devan teases you if you sided with Tamsin at the hold). Rivalries are written in, like the crew's bets. There are no meters or scores.
- **Tone rules carry over** from mission-control-voice §3:
  - short beats;
  - each person has a verbal habit;
  - sad things handled gently;
  - real science said like a person;
  - nods, never copies.

## 2. The five

### Mira "Castle" Castellanos, commander (she/her)
- **Shape:** a closed-off commander who opens up in small pieces and quietly takes the Pathfinder under her wing. You're about the age her son would be now.
- **The loss:** years ago she led an Antarctic station, and her son was the young engineer on her winter crew. She made the go call for a traverse in bad weather, and he didn't come back.
  - It's why she holds: why "Thursday's off" comes easily to her, and why she trusts the ground over the schedule.
- **How she cares:**
  - **Stern first, warm later.** Clipped and by the book early on. The warmth leaks out over weeks: a "get some sleep" that isn't an order.
  - **Lessons sideways.** She teaches through stories and small sayings from the station days, never lectures. You often get what she meant later, out in the field.
- **The reveal is hers, and it's earned.** Late in her thread, if you follow it that far, she asks a dangerous favour of you: a special outing for her. On it you **do** get stuck (a set event), out overnight. She stays on the radio all night, talks you through it and, in the end, talks you out. That's when she tells you about her son, in pieces between checks.
  - This time she gets her chance to bring someone home.
  - What the outing is, and where her thread starts, is still to design.
- **The circle closes at the finale.** You call the hold and she backs you.

### Devan Rao, pilot, climate
- **"The pool"** (from Theo and the old pool thread). An orbital-dynamics nerd who counts his coffees out loud and runs the crew's bets on your milestones. He loses most of them.
- A baby born on Earth after launch. Light-delayed videos come in, and the first word: he's sure it was "Mars."
- **Rivalry:** he wanted to try the pad anyway (scene 11b), and he needles whoever said hold.

### Tamsin Achterberg, engineer, geology
- **Open.** The first idea (a dismissed old theory proven right) was too close to an existing story and is cut. A hidden injury that could keep her from landing was explored, but nothing chosen yet felt right. Settle it in the character session.
- **Kept for now:** she keeps the orbiter's failing water recycler going, and she has a friendly feud with Kai ("the biology people").

### Joseph Mwangi, doctor, human exploration
- **"Fourteen kilometres behind you"** (from Kwame). Fusses over your sleep and your vegetables, prescribes walks you can't take, and runs your EVA distance on the orbiter treadmill.
- **Music as medicine.** Joseph plays guitar and left the good one on Earth. Joseph talks music with you, and some nights plays you a tune over the radio. The guitar you print for Joseph's future room becomes part of the story.

### Kai Tupou, biologist, life
- **"They think it's me who's down there"** (from Noor, the Firewatch heart). The orbiter's night watch, who can't sleep and tells you what Earth looks like out the window.
- A grandparent at home with dementia believes Kai is the one on the surface, so Kai reads them your days as Kai's own. It has good weeks, bad nights and a gentle ending, told over many quiet nights.
- The plush is from Kai's niece.

## 3. Earth
- **No cast of its own.** Bits of the old Mission Control world come in second-hand through the crew's family letters and gossip: the cat Biscuit, the night cleaner who beats everyone at chess, Public Affairs' merch disasters (the "MRAS" mug).
- **The old canon stays as a quarry:** `docs/mission-control-voice.md` (Ines, Theo, Sunny, Noor, Peg, Kwame, Ruth, Jasper, Pip). Its threads, event hooks and ideas parking lot are there to mine.

## 4. Furnishings and keepsakes
- The crew will land one day, so you ready their rooms.
- In the chats they let slip what they'd miss (Joseph's guitar, Kai's niece's plush).
- Some keepsakes are out on the plain, among the manifest's corrupted entries. These are low-stakes trips to find odds and ends, and once found you can print more. The rest you print.
- You place them in each person's future Quarters, and they notice on the helmet camera.
