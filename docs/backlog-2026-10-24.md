# Playtest list, 24 Oct: sorted into batches

This is the user's playtest list, sorted for discussion. The Missions 7–10 playtest is still to come.

## Decisions (24 Oct)
- **Batch A is done** except items 6 and 7, which wait for the model revamp:
  - Doors: each airlock now seals only its own inner door, so one open hatch no longer stops every auto door.
  - Night lighting: rooms get their own fill lights, and the outside keeps a moonlit floor instead of going black.
  - Items can't be placed in a doorway.
  - Storms, fog and snow now show through the windows.
  - The build bar has a thick, easy-to-grab scroll bar.
- **Batch B is done:**
  - The Field guide (with a switch for pop-up cards) is now in the pause menu and on the title screen, not just Mission Control.
  - The GAR-Y card appears the first time you walk up to GAR-Y in free play.
  - Mini GAR-Y is now **GAR-y**. Pat it: a hop, a spin and one of 12 captions.
  - GAR-Y names special rocks it finds on surveys (Gerald first) and keeps them beside its dock. All 12 are real Mars rocks, each with what it tells us.
  - The habitat voice has its own identity, not Subnautica's. It has a name you choose on the colony screen ("ARDEN" by default), talks in the first person, has 2–5 versions of every line, makes remarks at sunrise and sunset and when all is calm, and everything it says is captioned.
  - Mission Control sends radio messages from Earth after the real light delay (3–22 minutes each way, 26-month cycle), with Apollo-style Quindar beeps. Messages are held during a blackout.
  - Galley benches snap flush side by side, and small things can rest on a crew bunk.
- **Batch D is done.** Every kind of weather leaves a mark that wears off (scripts/weather_wear.gd):
  - **Dust** settles on each solar array: a little each sol, a lot in a storm. Each array loses up to 85% of its power at full dust, and it shows on the cells. It comes off when you brush it (E on the array), when a dust devil passes over (a toast says so), or with a lucky gust.
  - After a storm the **hull** is coated orange, fading over a few sols.
  - **Frost** forms overnight and sublimates through the morning on panels, hull and windows: heavy on the Ice Fields, light in the canyon's morning cloud and under the crater's ice clouds, and a trace on the plains. It costs up to 45% of solar power until it's gone.
  - The systems panel says what dust and frost are costing.
  - Dust and frost are on in Survival and Freedom, and in Missions from Mission 5. They're never on in Creative or in a mission Test.
  - **Visuals:**
    - A billowing dust front rises on the horizon while a storm is forecast, then closes in all round.
    - Big dust billows roll past during a storm.
    - Soft dust wisps replace the hard white streaks.
    - Dust devils are darker and denser.
    - Snowflakes are bigger, and they fill the air straight away.
  - Achievements are skipped for now: other changes come first.
- **Batch E (sleep only) is done.**
  - At a crew bunk, Z opens a small panel: 1, 2, 4 or 8 hours, or until sunrise, each showing the time you'd wake.
  - The screen goes dark and the clock runs about 120 times faster. Everything keeps happening.
  - A critical alert or an arriving dust storm wakes you straight away, and Esc wakes you early.
  - On waking you get a summary: battery before and after, and the alerts raised while you slept. ARDEN says goodnight and good morning.
  - You can't sleep outside, or with a critical alert up; ARDEN says why.
- **Batch C, wall and roof upgrades: done.** These are Structure tools in interior build (scripts/wall_upgrades.gd):
  - Regolith shielding (printable, 8 points per outside wall bay), a water wall (10), and roof shielding (8 per floor square, covering the whole room).
  - Sound insulation on walls between rooms. A Living Quarters insulated from its noisy neighbours sleeps well.
  - An outside wall bay holds either a window or shielding, not both.
  - Mission 7 uses shielding, unlocked in 7; insulation unlocks in 8. Old shield blocks and water wall panels in saves become wall upgrades, and they're retired from the build menu.
  - **Roof ladders** are an outside-wall upgrade. E climbs up and down, there's an accent rail round the roof edge, and the roof counts as outside.
  - **Picking things up:** E (or hold E, when E already does something) picks up any small item. A green or red ghost shows where it'll go; click or E puts it down, and Q puts it back. Saving or entering build mode puts it back first.
  - Batch C is done, except the science workbench, which is skipped for now.
- **C:** models are being revamped later, so item 19 waits. Shielding goes on the outer walls (agreed). Only small things can be picked up.
- **D:** dust settles on panels and the hull and **wears off over time** (wind, cleaning).
- **E:** sleep lets you choose how many hours, and wakes you on a critical alert. Achievements carry across saves, are fun but PG, and are designed with care since mechanics are still being added.
- **F:** write a design document first. Not literally every item, but every item is considered.

## Batch G · Missions playtest (24 Oct) and new ideas
The user's notes from playing Missions 5–10, with what I found and a proposed fix for each. Nothing here is built yet.

**G1 and G2 are done (24 Oct).** Items 25–34 below, with these differences from the proposals:
- **Mission 5:** the reactor is now 5 kW (it was 10; set in the balance sheet). Mission 5 allows one reactor and has a 7-sol planet-wide storm, and the Test checks the habitat stays above 15 °C as well as life support and health. One reactor with 4 arrays and a battery now fails; a reactor with plenty of batteries passes.
- **"How" lines:** they already existed on most steps. The collapsed panel now shows the whole current step, its How line and a "Now:" progress line. The redundancy step names each machine still missing its second.
- **Contact with Earth** (free play too) now needs a communications station in a Communications room as well as the powered mast outside.
- **Wall strips:** in remove mode, aiming at the wall where a thin wall-mounted item is picks that item.
- **The dossier** stays available in Mission Control for a base carried on from a finished campaign.

### G1 · Mission bugs (blocking or confusing)
25. **Mission 8: the Communications Mast is never delivered.** It's the "powered comms antenna" behind the crew's *Contact with Earth* need, but no mission unlocks it, so that need can't be met in Missions. **Fix:** deliver it in Mission 4, alongside the Communications room.
26. **Mission 8 should ask for something inside the Communications room, not just the room.** **Fix:** *Contact with Earth* needs a Communications room with a powered communications station inside it, plus the mast outside. Add it as a step: "Set up the Communications room: a station inside, the mast outside."
27. **Mission 7: "Choose a shelter room" doesn't say how.** **Fix:** rename the step to "Place a Storm Shelter Locker. The room it's in becomes the shelter," and add a "How" line under each step from now on.
28. **Mission 9: air reserve rack or oxygen reserve?** They do different jobs:
    - The **Oxygen Reserve** is breathing oxygen for when the oxygen generator fails.
    - The **Air Reserve Rack** holds stored air (mostly nitrogen) to re-pressurise a section after a breach.

    Neither can go in Living Quarters; both go in life support, storage, a corridor and so on. **Fix:**
    - Make each step say what the item is for and where it can go.
    - Check that a rack anywhere on the network can refill a breached section, so you never have to shrink the living quarters. If it can't, make it so.
29. **Mission 10's steps aren't clear.** **Fix:** each step gets a "How" line, e.g. "Two of every life-critical machine: oxygen generator, air recycler, water reclaimer, heating. When one breaks, the other carries on."
30. **When the mission panel is collapsed, the current step is cut off.** **Fix:** the collapsed panel shows the whole current step and its How line.
31. **The dossier disappears after you finish Mission 10 and keep playing.** **Fix:** keep it in Mission Control (and the pause menu) once the campaign is complete.
32. **The corridor light strip can't be removed.** **Fix:** find out whether it's a built-in corridor fixture or a placed item, then make it removable.

### G2 · Mission tuning
33. **Mission 5 is far too easy.** One compact reactor (10 kW) carries the whole storm. Proposed fix:
    - Make the storm planet-wide (a real thing: 2018 ended Opportunity) and longer: 8 sols at 90% cut.
    - Add a check: "Heating, the greenhouse and the lights stay on all storm."

    The base then needs more than one reactor can give. You'd have to combine the reactor, batteries charged before the storm, and switching off what isn't essential, which is the lesson.
34. **Mission 9's Test is slow.** It simulates 10 sols in 6-minute steps, but the breach is over in hours. **Fix:** test 3 sols.

### G3 · New ideas
35. **GAR-Y finds the ice himself.**
    - When landing, you see a rough ice map: broad likely and unlikely zones, from orbital radar (like SHARAD and MARSIS), not exact spots.
    - Give GAR-Y a new order, **Find ice**: he plans his own survey across the likely zone, drives a loop of stops, flags each find, and reports the best spot.
    - Flags show the richness, and the site plan shades the surveyed ground, so you can see the best places for ice extractors.
36. **Noise insulation as a wall upgrade, alongside radiation shielding (C).** Applied to a wall, not placed freely. It stops a noisy neighbour waking the Living Quarters. Also label noisy rooms ("Noisy: Power & Engineering, Life Support") in the build menu and on the room info.
37. **C opens the Crew screen.** It's free in explore mode.
