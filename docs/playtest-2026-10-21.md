# Playtest checklist: features added 21 Oct 2026

Tick each item and write a note for anything that looks or feels wrong. When you're done, say **"resume missions phase 2"** and paste in your notes.

## 1. Game modes and saves
- [ ] The main menu offers **Missions, Survival, Freedom and Creative**.
- [ ] **Survival:** the crew can come to harm.
- [ ] **Freedom:** you get warnings only.
- [ ] **Creative:** there is no simulation and no cargo limit.
- [ ] Missions saves are separate from the shared Free play saves (Survival, Freedom and Creative).
- [ ] Your old saves still load, listed under Free play.

## 2. Landing and the lander
- [ ] You pick a landing spot. The suggested spot is green.
- [ ] **Survival** lands somewhere inside the ellipse; **Freedom** and **Creative** land exactly on the spot you picked.
- [ ] You start inside the lander cabin. The cabin is dim and the hatch opens, giving you the "wonder" moment when you step out.
- [ ] You can't build on top of the lander or its keep-clear area.

## 3. Resource simulation
- [ ] Each room has its own temperature. Heaters and the heat pump (TCU) warm rooms; windows lose heat.
- [ ] Power: solar or the reactor, batteries, and night. When power runs short, life support is the last thing switched off.
- [ ] Air (O₂/CO₂), the water loop and food all drain and refill over time.
- [ ] Dust storms cut solar power.
- [ ] Warnings tell you the reason for each problem.
- [ ] Each site changes the numbers (for example, a cold site needs more heating).

## 4. Missions (Missions 1 and 2 only)
- [ ] **Mission 1, Landing Zones:** choose the landing ellipse and the base site.
- [ ] **Mission 2, Cargo Landing 1:** set up power and water while playing as a robot.
- [ ] **J** opens the brief. **T** runs a test and shows the report and graphs. Each test is saved as a version.
- [ ] The brief doesn't freeze the intro.

## 5. Build menus (new look)
- [ ] **Furnishing (B):** a bottom bar of tabs and cards with thumbnails, and the rotating 3D preview on the right.
- [ ] In furnishing: **Q/E** switch tabs, **1–9** pick a card, **Tab** toggles the cursor.
- [ ] The other HUD panels hide while you build and come back afterwards.
- [ ] The placement tag says either "Fits here" or why the item doesn't fit.
- [ ] **Site plan (Shift+B):** looks the same as the furnishing menu. The tool row floats above the bar, and 1–9 picks a card.
- [ ] The thumbnails look right: not too dark and not washed out.

## 6. Hold R to rotate
- [ ] In furnishing and with surface props: tapping R turns the item 15°, holding R spins it smoothly, and Shift+R turns it the other way.
- [ ] In the site plan: holding R keeps stepping 90°.

## 7. Partition walls (Furnishing → Structure tab)
- [ ] **Partition wall:** hold LMB and drag across a room's floor to draw 1.5 m panels.
- [ ] You can't draw a partition along an outer wall, in a corridor, in an airlock or in the vehicle bay.
- [ ] **Partition door:** aim at a panel to turn it into a door. **E** opens and closes the door.
- [ ] Furniture in the way blocks a panel. Each panel costs 60 kg of cargo.
- [ ] **Remove** deletes a panel. **Ctrl+Z** undoes your last change.
- [ ] Partitions save and load with your base, and they disappear when you delete their room.
- [ ] In the site plan, partitions show as dark lines and doors as orange lines.
